The puzzle

Simple rules. Real decisions.

No rotation and no skipping. New pieces arrive after all three in the tray are placed, so every move is part of a three-move plan.

  1. 01Drop

    Choose a piece from the tray and place it anywhere it fits on the 8×8 board.

  2. 02Fit

    Plan ahead. Pieces cannot overlap, rotate or hang over the edge.

  3. 03Clear

    Complete rows and columns together to create bigger clears and longer combos.
