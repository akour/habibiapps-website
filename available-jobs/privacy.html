Privacy Policy

This page describes the intended data handling for Habibi Jobs. The current early-access signup prototype stores profile information only in your browser and does not create a server-side account.

Information you provide

When production accounts are enabled, the service may collect your name, email address, password credentials through an authentication provider, job-search preferences, CV or profile information you choose to provide, and feedback such as roles opened, dismissed or applied to.

How information is used

Information is used to operate your account, personalize job discovery and ranking, deliver alerts, improve recommendations, provide support and protect the service from misuse.

Payments

Payment information will be handled by the selected payment provider. Habibi Jobs should not directly store complete card details.

Retention and deletion

You may request account deletion and removal of associated personal information, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to hello@habibiapps.com.

Before accepting paid customers, this policy must be reviewed and updated with the actual hosting, analytics, authentication, email and payment providers.
