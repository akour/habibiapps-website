Privacy Policy

Habibi Jobs uses the information you provide to operate your account and personalize job discovery.

Information we collect

This may include your name, email address, authentication information, job-search preferences, skills, seniority, industries, companies you exclude, subscription status, optional resume files, and feedback such as roles opened, dismissed or applied to.

Service providers

Account and profile data is processed through Supabase. Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record; Habibi Jobs does not store complete card details. Transactional and job-alert emails are delivered through Resend. Hosting and secure API functions are provided through Cloudflare.

How information is used

We use information to operate your account, discover, rank and filter jobs, deliver alerts, manage subscriptions, provide support, prevent misuse and improve recommendations. Resume files are stored privately and are not publicly accessible.

Retention and deletion

You may request account deletion and removal of associated personal information, subject to necessary legal and payment-record retention.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to hello@habibiapps.com.

This policy should receive legal review before a public paid launch.
