Questions

Before you sign up.

Does Habibi Jobs apply for me?

No. It finds and organizes relevant jobs. You stay in control of every application.

Will it find every job online?

No service can guarantee complete coverage. We scan supported career sites and discovery sources, and keep expanding coverage.

Can I change what I’m looking for?

Yes. Your roles, locations, remote preference and deal-breakers are part of your profile and can be updated.

Why not just use LinkedIn alerts?

LinkedIn is one source. Habibi Jobs also checks company career pages, removes repeated listings and filters results against your actual preferences.

Can it automatically submit applications?

No. High-volume auto-apply creates low-quality applications. The product is designed to help you find fewer, better opportunities.
