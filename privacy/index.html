Habibi Apps · Website privacy

Privacy notice.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This notice explains the optional Google Analytics measurement used on Habibi Apps’ public website. Product account areas and sign-in pages are not included in this measurement.

What we measure

Google Analytics is loaded only after you choose “Allow analytics.” When enabled, we measure page views and these link clicks:

  • Clicks to our Google Play listings, labelled by app.
  • Clicks on email contact links, recorded as a generic email-contact event.

Page-view reports contain the page path and title. The click events do not include the full link URL, your email address, or the contents of an email. We do not use these events to measure app installs or whether an email was sent.

Google Analytics and browser storage

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers and receive information such as page views, browser and device signals, and the link events described above. Google processes this information to provide analytics reports and according to its own terms and privacy disclosures. Read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you reject analytics, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded. Your choice is saved in this browser so the site can remember it. You can change that choice at any time below or from the “Privacy choices” link in the site footer.
