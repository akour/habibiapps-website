Serve the counter

Three orders a shift, each with its own ingredients and move limit. Finish early and the moves you saved become bonus points.

Earn your beans

Every shift pays out beans. Spend them on more moves, a re-grind of the counter, or a free pour that clears any piece you tap.

Quiet by design
