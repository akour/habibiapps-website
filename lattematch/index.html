Latte

Pull the shots, steam the milk, and keep the queue happy. Every shift brings three customer orders — a Qahwa Sada, a Karak, a Cold Brew — and each one asks for a set number of ingredients within a set number of moves.

Serve the counter

Three orders a shift, each with its own ingredients and move limit. Finish early and the moves you saved become bonus points.

Earn your beans

Every shift pays out beans. Spend them on more moves, a re-grind of the counter, or a free pour that clears any piece you tap.

Quiet by design

A warm palette instead of neon. Generative music that never loops the same way. No account, no sign-in, and it plays perfectly offline.
