A few useful details

Before we start.

Who do you work with?

Mobile app and game teams looking for help with app-store discoverability, website visibility in search and AI answers, launch planning, or ongoing growth experiments.

What does your AEO service include?

We review whether search engines can access the site, clarify the company and product information, improve answer-ready pages, check structured data and sitemap signals, then measure the search and referral data available. No provider can guarantee rankings or AI citations.

How is AEO different from ASO?

ASO improves how an app is found and chosen in app stores. AEO improves how your website and brand information can be understood and surfaced by search engines and AI answer systems.

Can we start with a focused project?

Yes. A store-page review, launch plan, or specific growth question is a useful starting point. We agree on scope and deliverables before work begins.

What should I send in the first email?

A link to your app or game, its launch stage, the markets you care about, and the problem you are trying to solve. Include your timing and budget if you have them.

Do you guarantee a growth result?

No. Outcomes depend on the product, market, and execution. We focus on clear recommendations, useful experiments, and the evidence available to your team.
